TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. Drag, touch, or use arrow keys or WASD to move.
2. Your ship fires energy bolts automatically.
3. Dodge asteroids, alien invaders, alien squads, and boss attacks.
4. Collect crystals for score and credits, and boosters for buffs such as rapid fire, triple shot, shield, magnet, speed, overdrive, and homing shots.
5. Survive waves through each LEVEL. A BOSS guards every 5th wave, and alien squads occasionally strike in formation. The debris field runs 50 waves deep — few pilots ever see what's waiting at the end.
6. Avoid damage to grow your combo multiplier!
7. Spend credits on SKINS for your ship and UPGRADES for stats. Some rare skins unlock only by reaching wave milestones or defeating bosses.
8. Play MULTIPLAYER cooperatively with a friend using a room code. No account is needed.
9. Your callsign saves your progress on this device. Switch pilots anytime from the main menu.
